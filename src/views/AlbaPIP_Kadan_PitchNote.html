The Problem

A PE fund manager in Singapore overseeing six portfolio companies across Indonesia, Vietnam, and Thailand is doing the same thing every Monday morning: chasing CEOs for weekly updates, building consolidation models in Excel, and calling CFOs to understand why a company missed its numbers. The information exists — in accounting systems, CRM platforms, bank accounts, and billing tools. It just isn't connected.

The Western solutions — Allvue, iLevel, Dynamo — cost £80,000–200,000 per year in licensing, are built for US and European fund structures, require 6-month implementations, and offer no meaningful AI. No product exists that is built for the Southeast Asian and Middle Eastern PE market, priced for it, or designed around how those fund managers actually operate.

The Solution

Alba PIP connects every system a portfolio company uses — accounting, banking, CRM, billing, market data, and HR — into a single intelligence layer, then puts AI agents to work across it. Not a dashboard. An analyst that works continuously, without being asked.

The AI agents investigate problems and cite their sources. They draft board packs in under 30 seconds. They send a morning portfolio briefing at 7am covering every company across every connected system. They alert the fund manager the moment a bank balance drops below a covenant threshold, or a purchase order exceeds the approval limit, or a key customer goes quiet in the CRM. This is what a team of three analysts does — running autonomously, at a fraction of the cost.
